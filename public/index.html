Why we run

One city.
One stadium.
One finish line.

Unity Run 2026 brings runners of every age and background to Barasat Stadium for a single shared morning — a fun run for families, a timed 5K for regulars, and a 10K for those chasing a personal best. Every registration helps fund community outreach and keeps the run free to attend as a spectator.

Whatever pace you keep, the start line doesn't ask — it just waits for you.

Venue

Barasat Stadium

Assembly from 5:30 AM at the main gate. Parking available on-site. Nearest station: Barasat Junction, 10 min walk.

Date & Time

20 September 2026

Flag-off at 6:00 AM sharp for the 10K, followed by the 5K and 3K. Medal ceremony to follow at the stadium grounds.

What's included

Bib, Tee & Medal

Every finisher receives an event T-shirt, a numbered bib, hydration on-route, and a finisher's medal at the line.
