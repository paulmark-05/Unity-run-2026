Why we run

One city.
One stadium.
One finish line.

Unity Run 2026 brings runners of every age and background to Barasat Stadium for a single shared morning — a chip-timed 10K and 6K with separate men's and women's prizes, and a 4K walk open to everyone. Every registration helps fund the Armed Forces Flag Day Fund.

Spectators are welcome, and free to watch from the stands.

Venue

Barasat Stadium

Spectators are welcome and free to watch from the stands.

Date & Time

Sunday, 20 September 2026

Flag-off at 6:00 AM sharp for the 10K, followed by the 6K. Medal ceremony on completion of the run, at the stadium.

All participants must assemble at the venue by 5:00 AM.

What's included

Bib, T-Shirt, Medal & Certificate

Runners receive a chip-timed bib, event T-shirt, medal, certificate, hydration on-route and breakfast. Walkers receive a T-shirt, certificate and breakfast.
