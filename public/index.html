Why we run

One city.
One stadium.
One finish line.

Unity Run 2026 brings runners of every age and background to Barasat Stadium for a single shared morning — a timed 6K with separate men's and women's prizes, and a 4K walk open to everyone. Every registration helps fund the Armed Forces Flag Day Fund.

Spectators are welcome, and free to watch from the stands.

Venue

Barasat Stadium

Spectators are welcome and free to watch from the stands.

Date & Time

Sunday, 27 September 2026

Flag-off at 6:00 AM for the 6K run, followed by the 4K walk. Medal ceremony on completion of the run and walk, at the stadium.

All participants must assemble at the venue by 5:00 AM.

What's included

Bib, T-Shirt & Medal

Runners receive a bib, event T-shirt, medal, hydration on-route and refreshments. Walkers receive a T-shirt, medal and refreshments.
